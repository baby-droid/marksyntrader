## Pair Cycle V2

After two consecutive parity-recovery losses, wait for four rises and buy PUT; then wait for two or three falls and buy CALL; then place two sequential Over 3 entries.

**Why:** The user confirmed this exact Pair V2 sequence.

**How to apply:** Preserve the stage order and continue through each configured stage regardless of whether the preceding trade wins or loses.

## Ahmed Differ Over/Under Cycle V1

After two consecutive parity-recovery losses, wait for four rises and buy PUT, then wait for two or three falls and buy CALL, then return to the main cycle.

**Why:** The user confirmed the two-loss trigger and the Rise/Fall order.

**How to apply:** Keep the V1 recovery separate from Pair V2; V1 does not add the two Over 3 stages.
