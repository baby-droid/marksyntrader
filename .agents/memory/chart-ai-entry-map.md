---
name: Chart AI Over/Under entry map
description: The reference-based strong entry digits and their scope in chart AI.
---

The chart AI uses a dynamic entry map for Over/Under: every digit on the winning side of the selected barrier is valid, ordered from farthest to nearest. The old strong/weak lists are only preference metadata; the shield digit is optional.

**Why:** A hard strong-digit/shield gate prevented valid qualifying markets from ever reaching the entry-point confirmation loop.

**How to apply:** Derive entries from the selected barrier (Over 3 → 4–9; Under 7 → 0–6), use the 50-tick distribution as the market gate, and use historical results only to select duration. Do not apply this map to unrelated groups such as Rise/Fall or Even/Odd.
