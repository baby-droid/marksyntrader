## Long-Term Goal

Rather than building a collection of fixed bots, build a **general-purpose visual automation platform**. Users (or AI assistants) can compose workflows from reusable blocks, import/export them as XML, extend the system through plugins, backtest strategies, monitor performance, and execute workflows through documented broker APIs. This architecture is more scalable, easier to maintain, and supports future additions without changing the core engine.
