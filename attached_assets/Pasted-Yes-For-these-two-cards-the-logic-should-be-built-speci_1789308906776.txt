Yes. For these two cards, the logic should be built specifically around **digit parity distribution + recent digit flow + multi-window confirmation**.

The important distinction is:

* **Card 1 = ODD bias → DIGITODD**
* **Card 2 = EVEN bias → DIGITEVEN**
* **1000 ticks = structural/statistical baseline**
* **50 ticks = short-term confirmation**
* **20 ticks = immediate pattern/flow confirmation**
* **10 ticks = precise entry trigger**

The 10-tick window should **not determine the overall bias**. It should only determine whether the current moment is suitable for entry.

```text
DIGITODD
```

```text
= 55.0%
```

Even:

```text
0 + 2 + 4 + 6 + 8
= 45.0%
```

This establishes a **longer-window Odd bias**.


to decide Odd or Even.


as a hierarchical confirmation system.
