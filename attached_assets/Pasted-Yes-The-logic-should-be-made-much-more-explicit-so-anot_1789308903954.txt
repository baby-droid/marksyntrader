Yes. The logic should be made much more explicit so another LLM can implement the **strategy engine, scanner, validation, signal generation, market routing, and execution rules** without having to infer missing pieces.

Below is the refined **logic-only specification** for the three directional cards.

# 1. CORE OBJECTIVE

Build a directional market-selection engine with three strategies:

### Card 1 — RISE

Trades **Rise/Call only**.

### Card 2 — FALL

Trades **Fall/Put only**.

### Card 3 — RISE/FALL BIAS

Can trade either **Rise or Fall**, automatically selecting the stronger direction from market bias, price flow, momentum, structure, and multi-window confirmation.

The engine should prioritize:

1. High-quality setups
2. Strong directional agreement
3. Low-chop markets
4. Multi-time-window confirmation
5. Market-specific behavior
6. Few but higher-quality trades
7. Capital preservation
8. Automatic market switching when conditions deteriorate

It must **not assume that a high score guarantees a winning contract**. A signal is only an entry candidate after all required filters pass.

---

# 2. SUPPORTED MARKET FAMILIES

The scanner should dynamically discover available markets and classify them into:

```text
1. 1s Volatility
2. Jump Indices
3. Plain Volatility Indices
4. Bear Markets
5. Bull Markets
```

The engine must not assume every market behaves identically.

Each market receives a classification:

```text
market_family
market_symbol
tick_speed
volatility_regime
directional_regime
data_quality
tradability
```

Example:

```text
Market:
Volatility 100 (1s)

Family:
1s Volatility

Regime:
Bullish trend

Volatility:
High

Chop:
Low

Rise suitability:
Excellent

Fall suitability:
Poor

Bias suitability:
Excellent
```

# 3. MARKET DISCOVERY ENGINE

The scanner starts by discovering active instruments.

For every discovered market:

```text
GET market metadata
        ↓
GET current price
        ↓
LOAD 1000 historical ticks
        ↓
START live tick stream
        ↓
BUILD rolling 1000-tick buffer
        ↓
CALCULATE indicators
        ↓
CLASSIFY market
        ↓
CALCULATE Rise score
        ↓
CALCULATE Fall score
        ↓
CALCULATE Bias score
        ↓
RANK market
```

A market cannot become tradable until its required historical buffer is available.

# 4. 1000-TICK BASELINE

The **1000-tick dataset is the long-term statistical baseline**.

It should not directly trigger an entry.

Its purpose is to determine:

* dominant direction
* long-term slope
* volatility
* trend strength
* directional persistence
* market stability
* recent regime relative to historical behavior
* whether the current 20/50/100-tick behavior is unusual

Calculate:

```text
1000T direction
1000T slope
1000T volatility
1000T high
1000T low
1000T range
1000T average movement
1000T upward movement %
1000T downward movement %
1000T alternation %
1000T persistence
```

The 1000-tick baseline acts as the **regime filter**.

---

# 5. SHORT-TERM WINDOWS

Use three entry windows:

```text
20 ticks
50 ticks
100 ticks
```

Their purposes are different.

### 20 ticks

Detect immediate flow.

```text
What is happening RIGHT NOW?
```

### 50 ticks

Detect short-term structure.

```text
Is the current move persistent?
```

### 100 ticks

Confirm the local trend.

```text
Is this more than a short burst?
```

Therefore:

```text
1000 = regime
100  = local trend
50   = confirmation
20   = entry flow
```

---

# 6. PRICE MOVEMENT CLASSIFICATION

Every consecutive tick should be classified:

```text
UP
DOWN
FLAT
```

For each window:

```text
up_count
down_count
flat_count
up_ratio
down_ratio
flat_ratio
```

Example:

```text
20 ticks:

UP   = 14
DOWN = 5
FLAT = 1

UP ratio = 70%
DOWN ratio = 25%
```

This is a strong short-term bullish flow.

---

# 7. DIRECTIONAL DOMINANCE

For each window calculate:

```text
directional_edge = abs(up_ratio - down_ratio)
```

Example:

```text
UP = 68%
DOWN = 27%

edge = 41%
```

This is much stronger than:

```text
UP = 53%
DOWN = 47%
```

The second condition should generally be rejected because the market is balanced.

Suggested minimum directional dominance:

```text
<55%      weak
55–59%    marginal
60–64%    usable
65–69%    strong
70%+      very strong
```

These are **filters/heuristics**, not guarantees. They should ultimately be calibrated from backtesting.

---

# 8. MULTI-WINDOW CONFIRMATION

A directional signal becomes stronger when:

```text
20T
50T
100T
```

agree.

Example:

```text
20T = UP 72%
50T = UP 65%
100T = UP 61%
```

Excellent alignment.

But:

```text
20T = UP 75%
50T = DOWN 57%
100T = DOWN 55%
```

should be rejected for Rise because the immediate move may be a temporary spike.

The engine should therefore distinguish:

```text
FULL ALIGNMENT
PARTIAL ALIGNMENT
CONFLICT
```

# 9. TREND STRUCTURE ENGINE

Do not rely only on percentage movement.

Analyze price structure.

## Rise structure

Look for:

```text
Higher High
Higher Low
Higher High
Higher Low
```

Example:

```text
100.00
100.20  HH
100.10  HL
100.35  HH
100.25  HL
100.50  HH
```

This indicates bullish continuation.

## Fall structure

Look for:

```text
Lower Low
Lower High
Lower Low
Lower High
```

Example:

```text
100.00
99.80   LL
99.90   LH
99.60   LL
99.70   LH
99.40   LL
```

This indicates bearish continuation.

---

# 10. MOMENTUM ENGINE

Calculate momentum over multiple windows.

For example:

```text
momentum20
momentum50
momentum100
```

Momentum should consider:

```text
price displacement
movement frequency
movement consistency
acceleration
```

A market moving upward is not necessarily strongly bullish if the upward movement is slowing rapidly.

---

# 11. ACCELERATION

Compare recent momentum against previous momentum.

Example:

```text
Previous 20T momentum = +0.04
Current 20T momentum  = +0.09
```

Acceleration is positive.

This strengthens Rise.

Conversely:

```text
Previous = +0.09
Current  = +0.02
```

Bullish momentum is weakening.

The Rise score should be reduced.

---

# 12. PERSISTENCE ENGINE

Measure how long directional movement survives.

Example:

```text
UP UP UP DOWN UP UP UP
```

has better bullish persistence than:

```text
UP DOWN UP DOWN UP DOWN
```

Calculate:

```text
average_up_run
average_down_run
maximum_up_run
maximum_down_run
current_run
```

A directional strategy should prefer persistent movement rather than alternating movement.

---

# 13. CHOP DETECTOR

This is one of the most important filters.

Detect:

```text
UP DOWN UP DOWN UP DOWN
```

or:

```text
DOWN UP DOWN UP DOWN UP
```

These conditions should reduce or completely block directional trades.

Calculate:

```text
alternation_rate
direction_changes
average_run_length
```

Example:

```text
20T:

UP/DOWN changes = 15
average run = 1.25
```

This is highly choppy.

Result:

```text
NO TRADE
```

---

# 14. REVERSAL DETECTOR

Do not immediately switch direction because of one opposite tick.

Example:

```text
UP UP UP UP UP DOWN
```

is still potentially bullish.

But:

```text
UP UP UP DOWN DOWN DOWN DOWN
```

indicates possible reversal.

Require confirmation from:

```text
20T
50T
100T
```

before declaring a reversal.

A reversal becomes credible when:

```text
20T direction changes
+
50T momentum weakens
+
100T structure breaks
```

# 15. RISE CARD LOGIC

The Rise card can **only execute Rise contracts**.

It should require:

### Regime

```text
1000T = bullish or neutral-bullish
```

### Short-term direction

```text
20T = UP
50T = UP
100T = UP
```

Prefer:

```text
UP ratio >= 60%
```

across the important windows.

### Structure

Require some evidence of:

```text
HH + HL
```

### Momentum

```text
momentum > 0
```

and preferably increasing.

### Persistence

```text
up runs > down runs
```

### Chop

```text
chop = LOW
```

### Counter-movement

Small counter-moves are acceptable.

Large repeated counter-moves are not.

# 16. RISE SCORE

Example scoring model:

```text
Rise Score =
    20% × 20T direction
  + 20% × 50T direction
  + 20% × 100T direction
  + 15% × 1000T regime
  + 10% × momentum
  + 5%  × structure
  + 5%  × persistence
  + 5%  × volatility quality
  - counter-move penalty
  - chop penalty
```

Example:

```text
20T = 91
50T = 84
100T = 81
1000T = 76
Momentum = 88
Structure = 90
Persistence = 86
Volatility = 80

Rise Score = 85+
```

Only then can it become a candidate.

# 17. FALL CARD LOGIC

Fall is the exact directional mirror.

Require:

```text
1000T = bearish / neutral-bearish

20T = DOWN
50T = DOWN
100T = DOWN

down ratio >= preferred threshold

Lower Low
Lower High
Lower Low

negative momentum

strong downward persistence

low chop
```

Reject when:

```text
20T DOWN
50T UP
100T UP
```

because the immediate decline may simply be a pullback.

# 18. FALL SCORE

Use:

```text
Fall Score =
    20% × 20T downward direction
  + 20% × 50T downward direction
  + 20% × 100T downward direction
  + 15% × 1000T bearish regime
  + 10% × negative momentum
  + 5%  × bearish structure
  + 5%  × persistence
  + 5%  × volatility quality
  - counter-move penalty
  - chop penalty
```

# 19. RISE/FALL BIAS CARD

This is the most flexible card.

It does not have a permanently assigned direction.

It calculates:

```text
Rise Score
Fall Score
```

and compares them.

Example:

```text
Rise = 87
Fall = 59
```

Therefore:

```text
BIAS = RISE
```

The engine may consider a Rise contract.

Another market:

```text
Rise = 58
Fall = 89
```

Therefore:

```text
BIAS = FALL
```

A third market:

```text
Rise = 72
Fall = 70
```

Result:

```text
NO TRADE
```

because there is insufficient directional separation.

---

# 20. BIAS SEPARATION

Do not simply choose the larger score.

Require a minimum difference.

```text
bias_gap = RiseScore - FallScore
```

Use absolute value:

```text
abs(RiseScore - FallScore)
```

Suggested interpretation:

```text
0–5      completely balanced
6–9      weak bias
10–14    usable bias
15–24    strong bias
25+      very strong bias
```

The Bias card should normally require a meaningful gap before trading.

# 36. POST-LOSS LOGIC

After one loss:

```text
DO NOT immediately reverse direction.
```

Instead:

```text
recalculate market
recalculate Rise score
recalculate Fall score
recalculate flow
recalculate structure
```

If the same direction remains strong:

```text
revalidate same direction
```

If the opposite direction becomes dominant:

```text
switch bias
```

If both become weak:

```text
STOP
```

# 37. THREE-LOSS PROTECTION

After 3 consecutive losses:

```text
STOP CURRENT MARKET
```

Then:

```text
clear active signal
reload/revalidate market state
scan alternative markets
```

The system should not simply increase stake to recover losses.

---

# 38. FOUR-LOSS PATTERN RESET

If the engine reaches 4 consecutive losses:

```text
invalidate current strategy state
reset virtual validation
reclassify market
switch market if necessary
```

Possible result:

```text
CURRENT MARKET = REJECTED
```

for a cooldown period.
